# Ops reporting and staff workflows

Migration `20261118100000_ops_workflows` extends Ops records without replacing Inventory, Water, Blog or Tender data. Existing Ops documents become Version 1 using their existing storage keys. The migration adds Inventory and Tender module grants, independent business management and finance permissions, standalone weekly tasks, monthly reporting fields, leave, scheduled submissions and password reset tokens.

Apply reviewed migrations to the intended database and regenerate Prisma before restarting the API. Run `npm run ops:backfill:weekly-tasks` against that same database to materialize tasks from existing submitted weekly records. It preserves existing tasks and their current statuses. Review database targeting before using either command. Local verification uses a separate database whose name ends in `_verify`; do not copy a development database into production.

The combined application no longer registers `ReportsModule` or `FinanceReportsModule`. Their controllers, report reminders and scheduled report emails stop after the updated backend restarts. Legacy report records are retained. The notification digest excludes old reporting notification types, including previously unread alerts. Other operational notifications and the public Blog API remain available.

The CEO has full Water, Blog, Tender and Inventory access. Staff need an individual view or manage module grant. Tender staff additionally see only bids where they are the account manager or an assigned resource. Assigning a tender grants its staff account Tender manage access if it has no existing grant. Existing tender assignees receive a grant in the migration. Payment Ledger retains its independent grant policy.

Business assignments support management, finance view and finance manage independently across multiple businesses. Home-business membership and Finance department membership do not grant financial access. Existing broad grants retain business visibility and need explicit financial permissions configured by the CEO. Amsol remains the protected core business `b0`.

Weekly submissions create persistent tasks, and weekly reports update linked task outcomes. Staff weekly pages list personal submissions and drafts across reporting weeks, with forms opened through New plan, New report or Resume draft. Viewing submissions and leaving the list do not mount an editor or trigger unsaved-change warnings. Dirty forms confirm dismissal and navigation. Scheduling and attachments remain available within the forms.

Staff can append tasks to their own submitted plan until its weekly report is submitted. The append action validates ownership, business access, duplicate identities and the current version, preserves earlier snapshots in audit history and creates persistent tasks without resetting existing task progress. An updated plan returns to awaiting CEO review. Previously generated email snapshots remain intact. Report editors include newly appended plan tasks even when resuming an earlier draft. Returned reports can be corrected before further tasks are added to their plan.

The staff department workspace shows permitted department projects, their tasks across teams, available standalone work for staff in that department, explicitly department-assigned requests, submitted monthly reports and shared department/project documents. Its task queue and Tasks page use the same department scope. Personal drafts and submission attachments stay out of the shared dashboard. This view uses the existing server-filtered workspace and does not grant finance, business or module permissions.

The Monthly Reports screen retains existing reports and supports monthly and ad hoc submissions, task outcome snapshots, attachments and recorded leave. Drafts are private. Leave snapshots include only dates overlapping the report period and are retained after submission. Ordinary documents support selected-staff sharing, original downloads and numbered version history; submission attachments retain their original content.

Staff can schedule a saved weekly plan, weekly report or monthly report in Africa/Nairobi time. A 30-second worker claims due submissions and validates the latest draft under the staff member's current authority, including when their browser session has ended. The worker records completion or failure. Staff can reschedule or cancel pending schedules. Draft changes saved before submission are included. Submitted snapshots and CEO email snapshots are independent: personal scheduling does not send an immediate individual email to the CEO.

Reminder emails use the existing SMTP configuration and `OPS_EMAIL_JOBS_ENABLED=true`, and are attempted during the final hour before submission. In-app schedule notices remain available without SMTP. Keep the backend process running for automatic submission and reminders. Consolidated weekly CEO emails retain the existing weekly settings, delivery claims and uncertain-send handling.

Forgot password uses `/ops/auth/forgot-password` and `/ops/auth/reset-password`, with links to `OPS_FRONTEND_URL/login?reset=...`. Reset tokens are hashed, expire after one hour and are claimed once. Resetting a password revokes Ops sessions and outstanding reset/setup links. Public responses do not reveal whether an account exists.

The CEO can edit staff details and the linked sign-in email from Staff or Settings > Accounts. `PATCH /ops/staff/:id` updates these together, rejects duplicate emails and revokes existing Ops sessions and setup/reset links when the email changes. Resend invitation creates and emails a new setup link to the current address. `DELETE /ops/accounts/:id` removes Ops access and permissions while retaining the staff record, reports and shared AIMS identity. Deleting an unused Staff entry removes its account and grants together; linked work or reporting history produces an explicit retention message. CEO and System Admin accounts cannot be edited or deleted through these staff actions.

The connected verification script is `test/ops-workflows.verify.ts`. Start `test/ops-browser-server.ts` with a local `OPS_TEST_DATABASE_URL` ending in `_verify` and `OPS_WEEKLY_TEST_EMAIL=1`. Its SMTP and clock controls are local fixtures. The workflow script checks module grants, scoped inventory queries, business finance isolation, assignment visibility, weekly tasks, leave, reminders, automatic submission after logout, document versions and password recovery.
